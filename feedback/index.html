Alamin pa · Know more

The fight was fiction. The revolution was not. If the game left you curious, start here:

  • The Revolt of the Masses, Teodoro Agoncillo (1956). The classic account of the Katipunan and Bonifacio.
  • Pasyon and Revolution, Reynaldo Ileto (1979). How ordinary Filipinos understood the revolution.
  • Kartilya ng Katipunan, Emilio Jacinto. The teachings every member swore to. Short enough to read today.
  • Filipino Martial Culture, Mark V. Wiley (1997). The history and masters of arnis, eskrima and kali.
  • Visit the Museo ng Katipunan at the Pinaglabanan Shrine in San Juan, and the National Historical Commission of the Philippines.
